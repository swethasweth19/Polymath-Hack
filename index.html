One minute, in my own words

What makes you want to build like a polymath?

I build like a polymath because real problems never respect subject boundaries. My waste-classification app, Trash to Tech, needed machine learning, sustainability thinking, and stage presentation skills. SmartMirror-Vox combined Raspberry Pi hardware, speech APIs, and Python backend design. Each project taught me that the best solutions appear where disciplines overlap. I enjoy moving between NLP, IoT, data analytics, and storytelling, because every field sharpens how I think in the others. I want to keep crossing those lines, learning, and shipping things people use. Polymath building isn't about knowing everything; it's about being curious enough to learn whatever the problem demands.

Where my work overlaps

Trash to Tech sits between machine learning and data. SmartMirror-Vox sits between hardware and speech. My best ideas start in the middle.
